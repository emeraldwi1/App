# Ohio Childcare: Staff Files + Training Tracking (OPR) Checklist (2026)

This post is informational and operational, not legal advice. Confirm exact training and qualification requirements for your program type in the OAC/ODJFS guidance.

## What “good” looks like in an inspection

When staff records are solid, you can answer three questions immediately:

- Is this person eligible to be in ratio today?
- What is expiring next (and when)?
- Where is the proof stored?

## The staff-file checklist (system, not a binder)

For each staff member, track four fields for every requirement:

- `Requirement`
- `Status` (missing / submitted / active / expiring)
- `Due/renewal date`
- `Proof location` (link/path)

## OPR: keep one source of truth

If your team uses the Ohio Professional Registry (OPR), treat it as a structured data source:

- ensure each staff member profile is complete
- record OPR identifiers in your staff profile
- keep a “last verified” date

## Naming conventions prevent chaos

Pick one format and never change it:

- `STAFF_Last_First__BackgroundCheck__YYYY-MM-DD.pdf`
- `STAFF_Last_First__Training__Topic__YYYY-MM-DD.pdf`
- `STAFF_Last_First__Medical__YYYY-MM-DD.pdf`

## What to do this week

- Pick your canonical storage path (no duplicates).
- Build a “Next 30 days expiring” view.
- Assign one owner for renewals follow-up.
