# Ohio Childcare: Safe Sleep and Infant Care Compliance Basics (2026)

This post is informational and operational, not legal advice. Confirm safe sleep requirements for your program type in the OAC/ODJFS guidance and align your written policy with your actual classroom practice.

## Why safe sleep compliance “drifts”

Safe sleep issues usually appear because daily practice slowly changes:

- extra blankets appear
- positioning changes based on “what worked yesterday”
- documentation becomes inconsistent

In an inspection or incident review, “we usually do it right” is not enough. You need consistent practice and proof.

## The operational safe-sleep checklist

Use a checklist at setup and spot-checks:

- approved sleep surface in good condition
- sleep area clear (no extra items)
- infant placed according to policy and requirements
- supervision checks performed on a consistent cadence
- any exceptions documented and escalated

## Documentation habits that protect you

- record sleep checks in a consistent place (and time-stamp them)
- note any deviations immediately
- document parent communications when relevant

## What to do this week

- Run a 10-minute “sleep space audit” at opening and after nap.
- Train: “if it’s not allowed, remove it immediately.”
- Verify your written policy matches your real routine.
