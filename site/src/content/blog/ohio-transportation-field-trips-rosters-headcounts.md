# Ohio Childcare: Transportation + Field Trip Rosters and Headcounts (2026)

This post is informational and operational, not legal advice. Confirm transportation requirements for your program type in the OAC and ODJFS guidance.

## The risk profile

Transportation and off-site trips compress multiple risks into one event:

- supervision in unfamiliar spaces
- moving groups across thresholds (bus/van, parking lots, entrances)
- communication with guardians
- emergency preparedness away from the building

## The roster system (simple and defensible)

Use one roster for the entire trip that includes:

- child names
- assigned group/classroom
- staff assigned to the trip
- emergency contact access (or how to access it securely)

## Headcount points (non-negotiable)

Write the headcount moments into your routine:

- before loading
- after loading
- on arrival
- before departure
- after return

## Documentation you should keep

- trip plan (where, when, purpose)
- roster + headcount confirmation notes
- incident log (even if “none”)
- any guardian communications (schedule changes, delays)

## What to do this week

- Create a one-page transportation checklist.
- Add it to your “ready-to-go” folder with rosters and emergency info access instructions.
