# Ohio Childcare: Medication Administration Permissions and Logs (2026)

This post is informational and operational, not legal advice. Confirm medication rules for your program type in the OAC and your program policies.

## What inspectors are looking for

Medication findings usually come down to:

- missing written permission
- unclear storage practices
- incomplete logs (who, what, when, dosage)
- staff not following the same process every time

## The repeatable medication workflow

1. **Intake:** verify the medication is labeled and the permission/authorization is complete.
2. **Storage:** store securely, separated as needed, and consistent with temperature requirements.
3. **Administration:** verify child identity, confirm dosage and time, administer, and observe for reaction.
4. **Documentation:** log immediately.
5. **Return/disposal:** return to guardian or dispose per policy; document exceptions.

## The log fields that matter

- Child name
- Medication name
- Date/time administered
- Dosage and method
- Staff initials/signature
- Notes (refusal, spit-up, reaction)

## What to do this week

- Standardize one medication form and one log format.
- Audit: “Do we have any meds stored with missing permission?”
- Train staff on “no permission, no administration” (no exceptions).
