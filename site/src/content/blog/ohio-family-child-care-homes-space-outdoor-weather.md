# Ohio Family Child Care Homes: Space + Outdoor Play Weather Limits (2026)

This post is informational and operational, not legal advice. Confirm requirements directly in the OAC and any current ODJFS guidance.

## Why these topics matter

Space and outdoor play expectations are easy to misunderstand because they look "obvious" until an inspection asks:

- What counts as **usable** indoor/outdoor space?
- What are the **temperature limits** for outdoor play in Ohio rules?
- What documentation shows you followed the rule consistently?

## Indoor and outdoor usable space (quick reference)

Ohio's family child care home rules include minimum usable space:

- **Indoor usable space:** 35 square feet per child
- **Outdoor play space:** 60 square feet per child

Practical tip: maintain a simple floor plan note that lists:

- rooms counted as usable child space
- excluded areas (kitchens, storage, blocked-off rooms, etc.)
- capacity assumptions you are using

That one-page note prevents repeated measurement debates during inspections.

## Outdoor play: the temperature range in the rule (and who it applies to)

Ohio's rule on outdoor play for family child care homes includes this temperature range:

- "Suitable weather" is at a minimum **twenty-five to ninety degrees Fahrenheit**.

The same rule also ties outdoor play to attendance patterns, including:

- infants over **twelve months** of age, toddlers, preschool and school-age children
- in attendance **four or more consecutive daylight hours**
- an exemption if only one staff member is present and there is an infant under twelve months in care

Practical tip: add a recurring daily field in your routine log:

- `Outdoor play offered? (Y/N)`
- `Temperature / Weather reason if N`

This is not paperwork for paperwork's sake. It is your evidence that your practice matches the rule.

## A simple compliance checklist you can use tomorrow

- Confirm your **space math**: indoor and outdoor square footage per child.
- Identify the **usable areas** and document them once.
- Train staff/helpers on the **25F-90F** outdoor play expectation.
- Keep a minimal log line when outdoor play is not offered due to weather.
