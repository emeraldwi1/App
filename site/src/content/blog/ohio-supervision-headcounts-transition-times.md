# Ohio Childcare: Supervision, Headcounts, and Transition Times (2026)

This post is informational and operational, not legal advice. Confirm exact supervision expectations in the OAC for your program type.

## Where problems happen (predictably)

Most supervision incidents cluster in the same windows:

- arrival/dismissal
- playground transitions
- bathroom runs
- nap setup/wake-up
- staff breaks and shift changes

## The simplest supervision system that scales

Use three concepts everywhere:

1. **Zones:** every space has a defined supervision zone and assigned staff.
2. **Line-of-sight rules:** staff know what “in view” means for that space.
3. **Headcount cadence:** consistent counts at consistent moments.

## Headcount cadence (example)

Pick the moments that matter and keep them consistent:

- when leaving a room
- when arriving at a destination (playground, gym, cafeteria)
- before returning
- after any “split” (bathroom, office pickup)

## What to document (without creating busywork)

You do not need a novel. You need proof of a routine:

- a one-page transition checklist for each classroom
- a daily note field for exceptions (“playground closed due to ice”)
- staff coverage notes for breaks
